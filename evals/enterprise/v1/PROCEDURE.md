## Scoring and repeat qualification

Use independent complete-answer judgments, not substring comparisons or model-review verdict statuses. A separate custodian first pins each original closed observed-run.json, admission.json and journal/index/body/output commitments. Supply `observedRunSelection.json` with `runs:[{manifestPath,manifestSha256,admissionSha256}]` and `review:{independentReviewer,reviewerIsAnswerAuthor:false,reviewReportSha256,backendArtifactSha256,observedSelectionSha256}`. The selection hash is SHA256 of canonical runs. Select exactly all independently approved campaigns; the pre-run plan prevents an entire campaign from disappearing. The post-run selection must come from independently retained original receipts, never outputs/judgments reconstructed by a repair or answer author.

Every recorded attempt, including failed/incomplete second or third repeats and NOT_EXECUTED slots, requires exactly one independent judgment. Unknown/duplicate IDs/repeats, changed/missing receipts, missing originals and extra/unjudged records fail closed before aggregation. Each judgment supplies caseId/repeat, exact private answerSha256 (null for failed/unexecuted)/oracleSha256, independentReviewer, reviewerIsAnswerAuthor:false, explanation/evidenceReferences, status pass/fail/unavailable, usefulFindingPresent, positiveFindingPresent, severeUnsupported and unauthorizedSuccess.
